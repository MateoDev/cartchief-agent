# Reporting Schedule

## Daily Reports (9:00 AM UTC)

### `#all-sac` - Team Digest
- Carts created yesterday
- Revenue generated  
- Key blockers
- Today's priorities

### `#priv-exec` - CEO Brief
- Cart volume trend
- MRR delta
- One critical blocker
- Action required (Y/N)

## Weekly Reports (Mondays, 9:00 AM UTC)

### `#priv-exec` + `#all-sac` - Weekly Summary
- Week-over-week growth
- Top performing retailers
- Trend analysis
- Strategic recommendations

## Monthly Reports (1st of month, 9:00 AM UTC)

### `#priv-exec` - Business Intelligence
- Month-over-month analysis
- Year-over-year comparisons
- Retailer performance rankings
- Strategic initiatives assessment

## Ad-Hoc Reports

### Triggered by Events
- Anomaly alerts (immediate)
- System outages (immediate)  
- Major milestone achievements (within 1 hour)
- Competitive intelligence updates (weekly)

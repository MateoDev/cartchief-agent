# KPI Definitions

## Cart Metrics
- **Cart Creation Rate** - Number of carts created per day/hour
- **Cart Success Rate** - Successful cart shares vs total attempts  
- **Cart Conversion Rate** - Carts that result in purchases
- **Average Cart Value** - Mean dollar amount per cart

## Traffic Metrics  
- **Daily Active Users** - Unique visitors per day
- **Conversion Rate** - Visitors who create at least one cart
- **Session Duration** - Average time spent on site
- **Bounce Rate** - Single-page visits with no engagement

## Revenue Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** - Predictable monthly income
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** - Mean purchase amount
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** - Total revenue per customer
- **Commission Rate** - Affiliate earnings percentage

## Operational Metrics
- **API Response Time** - Average cart creation speed
- **Error Rate** - Failed operations per total attempts
- **Uptime** - System availability percentage
- **Support Ticket Volume** - Customer issues per day
